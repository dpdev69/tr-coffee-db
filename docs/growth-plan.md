# V2 growth plan

Prepared 2026-09-14. Proposed experiments, not completed acquisition work. No community posts, emails, direct messages, invitations, sponsorships or campaigns have been sent. Obtain explicit authorization before communicating with other people or publishing campaigns.

## Operating targets

The owner's milestones are **100 → 500 → 1,000 confirmed subscribers**, not promises or launch forecasts. Count consenting Kit subscribers after the configured confirmation process; count neither form submissions nor imported contacts as confirmed new subscribers. Do not require an account or quiz to subscribe.

| Milestone | Focus | Evidence to review before the next stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 100 | Validate the clear weekly promise and deliver several useful editions. | Source-attributed confirmed signups, real outbound clicks, reasons for unsubscribing, repeat email clicks/visits across at least four weekly editions. |
| 100 to 500 | Repeat the best acquisition channel and improve relevance/editorial selection. | Consistent acquisition without invented deals, channel-level signup conversion, returning readers, roaster coverage and freshness. |
| 500 to 1,000 | Scale channels that bring engaged readers and demonstrate useful traffic to roasters. | Engaged subscriber growth rather than list size alone; per-roaster/coffee outbound traffic; editorial workload; retention and complaints. |

For traffic planning, 100 confirmed signups require 5,000 qualified visits at 2% conversion, 2,000 at 5%, or 1,000 at 10%. These are arithmetic scenarios, not expected conversion benchmarks. Measure the actual funnel before spending on acquisition. Opens may be affected by email-client privacy behavior; assess email clicks, attributable return visits and downstream roaster clicks alongside Kit opens.

## Measurement and independence

Track unique visitors, confirmed subscribers, visitor-to-subscriber conversion, weekly net subscriber growth, outbound clicks by coffee/roaster/source, privacy-safe return visits, Kit opens and clicks, unsubscribes and complaints. Report sources and periods; a bot click or duplicate submission must not become evidence of demand.

Use campaign tags on acquisition links and the canonical outbound redirect path on coffee links. Separate provider-confirmed signup success from a submitted form. Never put emails, full subscriber identifiers or free-form taste answers into analytics URLs/events.

Normal roaster inclusion stays free. Featured placements, if later enabled, must be labeled “Sponsorlu” and separate from organic sorting. No promised placement, commission, purchase attribution or commercial relationship exists merely because a roaster appears in the catalog.

## Personalization recommendation

**Do not begin the full personalization phase yet.** The current work establishes catalog/event trust and a measurable acquisition/retention loop. Subscriber thresholds alone are insufficient: the later gate also needs repeated discovery behavior, voluntary preference/feedback input, and evidence that recommendations improve outcomes. No such user-validation evidence has been collected in this document.
